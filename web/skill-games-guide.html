🧠⚡ SKILL • DABHOUSIE™ SPECIALS (SOLO • MULTIPLAYER • TEAM)

DabHousie™ Skill Games & Terminology Guide

While Classic Tambola, Housie & 90-Ball Bingo are multiplayer luck games where you can join as an individual or with friends,
DabHousie™ Skill Specials transform the 3×9 grid into a progressive curriculum of rules, spatial memory, column logic, mental math, and reflex speed — supporting Solo Practice, Live Multiplayer, and Team Co-Op.

🎲 Classic Luck Multiplayer vs. 🧠⚡ DabHousie™ Skill Specials

🎲 Classic Bingo, Tambola & Housie (Luck • Multiplayer)

Classic 90-Ball Bingo is a multiplayer social game by design — more players in a room make every race for Early 5, Lines, and Full House more exciting. Anyone can join a room solo-friendly (no team required) or bring friends and colleagues.

🧠⚡ DabHousie™ Specials (Skill • Solo, Multiplayer & Team)

Every player faces deterministic 3×9 cognitive, memory, math, or reflex challenges where outcomes are determined by accuracy (+10 pts), penalties (-3 pts), and millisecond reaction time. Practice anytime in Solo Mode, compete in Live Multiplayer, or collaborate in Team Arenas.

📖 Official DabHousie™ Skill Terminology & Core Mechanics

Every DabHousie™ Skill Special is built on the mathematical invariants of a balanced 3×9 ticket. Understanding these core terms gives you a decisive edge across all levels:

⚡ NeuroWave™ Column Spotlight

Our signature pre-round memory sweep. Before calling begins, a golden spotlight illuminates the active quadrant one column at a time (1.8 seconds per column), giving your brain a structured visual snapshot before cells flip into hidden [?] mystery tiles.

🔲 3×3 Spatial Quadrants (Q1 • Q2 • Q3)

To make spatial memory ergonomic, DabHousie™ partitions the 3×9 ticket into three balanced 3×3 quadrants, each guaranteed to hold 5 numbers and 4 blanks:
• Q1 (Left 3×3): Cols 1–3 (Numbers 1–29)
• Q2 (Middle 3×3): Cols 4–6 (Numbers 30–59)
• Q3 (Right 3×3): Cols 7–9 (Numbers 60–90)

📐 Column Decade & Ascending Logic

Every column on a 3×9 ticket is strictly bound to its decade (Col 1 = 1–9, Col 2 = 10–19 … Col 9 = 80–90), and numbers within any column always ascend from top row to bottom row. Even if you only remember which cells had numbers, you can logically deduce where a called number belongs!

🎯 Decoy-Weighted Draw Pool & Freeze Penalty

In FlashHousie™, the caller draws from a pool combining your true quadrant numbers with smart same-decade decoys (e.g., 7 balls for Flash 5, 14 for Flash 10, 20 for Flash 15). Correct hits score +10 pts; tapping a blank or wrong cell deducts -3 pts and freezes your board for 3 seconds.

🎓 Step 1 — Level 0 Foundation & Math Suite (Natural Learning Path)

In the natural educational order (Make & Fix → Memory & Reason), Level 0 teaches players how the 3×9 grid is constructed before they play blindfolded memory rounds in Level 1:

🎓 Level 0A: MakeHousie™ (Ticket Rule Builder)

Place 15 generated numbers into their valid column decades (1–9, 10–19 … 80–90), vertical ascending order, and 5-per-row balance. Includes Easy (guided) and Complex (un-bucketed pool) modes.

🔍 Level 0B: FixHousie™ (Spot the Mistake)

Inspect a pre-filled 3×9 ticket and spot 1, 3, or 5 intentional rule bugs: wrong column decade, flipped vertical sequence, duplicate numbers, or >90 balls.

➕ Level 0C: MathHousie™ (Formula-to-Grid Hunt)

Solve live arithmetic formulas above the board (e.g., 67 + 3 = ??, 14 × 5 = ??), jump straight to the matching decade column, and tap the answer on the 3×9 grid.

🧮 Level 0D: SumHousie™ (Quadrant Rapid Sum)

Add all 5 numbers inside an active 3×3 quadrant (Q1, Q2, or Q3) before the timer expires and select the exact sum from 4 smart multiple-choice options.

⚡ Step 2 — Level 1 (Open Now): FlashHousie™ 5 • 10 • 15

Once you know the 3×9 column rules, put your spatial memory and logical reasoning to the test in FlashHousie™ — playable right now in Solo Practice or Live Multiplayer:

1. NeuroWave™ Column Sweep

Before calling begins, the proprietary NeuroWave™ Column Spotlight sweeps across the active quadrant one column at a time for 1.8 seconds per column, then masks all numbers as [?] mystery tiles.

2. Flash 5 • Flash 10 • Flash 15

Choose Flash 5 (1 Quadrant • 5 balls), Flash 10 (2 Quadrants • 10 balls with a 5s memory lock-in pause between quadrants), or Flash 15 (All 3 Quadrants • 15 balls).

3. Precision Scoring & Reaction Speed

Each correct recall awards +10 pts. Tapping an uncalled or decoy cell triggers a -3 pt penalty and a 3-second board freeze. Ties are broken by average reaction time and total round time.

🚀 Step 3 — Levels 2–5 Progressive Skill Roadmap

Levels unlock sequentially as players master each skill tier across Solo, Multiplayer, and Team Co-Op modes:

📏 Level 2: RowHousie™ 1 • 2 • 3

Horizontal 1–90 row memory and deduction across full 9-column rows (5 numbers + 4 blanks per row).

⚡ Level 3A: FastTap™ Housie (Fastest Finger)

All players receive the exact same visible 3×9 ticket and race to tap called numbers first for Fastest 5, 10, 15, and Full House.

🧩 Level 3B: SwapHousie™ (Shuffle & Swap)

All 15 numbers and 12 blanks are scrambled across the 3×9 grid. Drag and swap tiles to restore valid column decades, vertical ascending order, and 5-per-row balance in the fewest moves.

🎯 Level 4: BlastHousie™ (45° Arcade Shooter)

Slide your horizontal turret and tilt ±45° to snipe called numbers through blank lanes while clearing bigger bottom-row blockers first.

🏗️ Level 5: StickHousie™ (Gravity & 3–6 Player Team Co-Op)

Stack rolling number balls and 12 structural dummy boxes into the 3×9 frame without letting heavier balls crush smaller ones below. Play solo or in 3–6 player Team Co-Op (up to 2 teammates per quadrant)!
