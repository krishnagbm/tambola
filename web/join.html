Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my game code?

Your game organizer or host will share a 6-character code (e.g. 14A3F7) via WhatsApp, email, chat, or by projecting it on a TV or screen.

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No. DabHousie runs 100% in your mobile or desktop web browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge). No app store downloads or sign-ups required.

Is joining as a player free?

Yes! Joining and playing in any DabHousie game is always 100% free for players and guests.

How does winning work?

As numbers are drawn, tap them on your digital ticket to dab them. When you complete a pattern (like Early 5, Lines, or Full House), tap Claim Prize for instant automated validation.
