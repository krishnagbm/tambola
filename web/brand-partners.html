How the Brand Partner Program Works

From promotional campaign setup to customer redemption in six transparent steps.

STEP 1

Brand creates an offer

Register your promotional reward, brand landing page URL, redemption rules, and target region (recommended 10-reward pilot).

STEP 2

DabHousie distributes the offer through games

Event organizers select your brand offer for winning patterns such as Early 5, Row Lines, or Full House.

STEP 3

Players compete for the reward

Engaged players see your brand logo and offer title throughout the live multiplayer game session.

STEP 4

Winner receives the reward

Verified winners receive your promotional reward in their Rewards Wallet and via winner notification email.

STEP 5

Winner visits the brand's landing page

Winners and Hall of Fame visitors click directly through to your designated website, app, or brand landing page.

STEP 6

Brand gets exposure and potential customer activity

Measure offer views and landing-page engagement, and use redemption data where available, before scaling your next campaign batch.

Brand Marketing Partner FAQ

Common questions from marketing managers, growth teams, and promotional sponsors.

Q: Do we have to provide traditional gift cards?

No. Brands can provide gift vouchers, discount codes, promotional offers, free products/services, trials or other customer incentives.

Q: How many rewards do we need to provide?

We recommend starting with a small 10-reward pilot.

Q: Can we target a specific country?

Yes. Offers can be configured for specific countries or regions, subject to the brand's redemption terms.

Q: Where does the winner go?

The reward can link directly to your designated website, app or promotional landing page.

Q: Can the offer have an expiration date?

Yes. Expiration and redemption restrictions can be specified when registering the offer.

Q: Can we use unique voucher codes?

Yes. The platform supports unique codes per winner as well as shared promotional codes.
