🎲 The Classic Game of Housie

Housie is the beloved social game played across community halls, social clubs, pubs, and family parties in the UK, Australia, New Zealand, and beyond.

Played with 90 numbered balls (1 to 90), players purchase or receive 3×9 tickets containing 15 numbers. As the caller announces each number, players mark their cards until someone completes a winning line or a Full House!

🎫 Housie Ticket Anatomy (3×9 Grid)

A standard Housie ticket consists of 3 rows and 9 columns (27 cells in total):

  • Every row has exactly 5 numbers and 4 blank squares (total 15 numbers per ticket).
  • Numbers in each column are neatly grouped by numerical decade (1–9, 10–19, 20–29 ... 80–90).
  • All numbers in each column appear in ascending order from top to bottom.

🏆 Standard Housie Winning Patterns

In traditional and digital Housie games, winners are crowned across multiple stages of play:

⚡ EARLY FIVE

Early 5 (Quick 5)

The first player to mark off any 5 numbers across their ticket.

🎯 FOUR CORNERS

Corners

The 4 extreme corner numbers: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, and bottom-right.

🥇 LINE 1

First Line

All 5 numbers marked off on the top horizontal line.

🥈 LINE 2

Second Line

All 5 numbers marked off on the middle horizontal line.

🥉 LINE 3

Third Line

All 5 numbers marked off on the bottom horizontal line.

👑 FULL HOUSE

Full House (Housie!)

All 15 numbers marked on the ticket. The ultimate championship prize of the game!
