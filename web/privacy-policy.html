Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026 • Effective Date: September 15, 2026
Digital App Studio

Welcome to DabHousie ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Digital App Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information collected through our web application at https://www.dabhousie.com and associated mobile services (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Google Authentication (OAuth): When you sign in using Google, we receive your basic public profile information provided by Google (your name, email address, and profile picture URL). We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Display Name and Avatar Key: You can customize your in-game player name and choose an avatar emoji or character.
  • Organizer & Event Data: When hosts schedule or create a game, we store event metadata such as game title, scheduled start time, capacity limits, and chosen prize configurations.

B. Anonymous / Guest Play Information

Guests joining games via invite codes or links are not required to create an account. We assign a pseudonymous session identifier stored locally in browser storage to preserve your game ticket and live claims during the session.

C. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device, browser type, and operating system version.
  • Game logs, including drawn numbers, ticket numbers, and timestamped prize claim assertions (used strictly to verify winner validity).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate game organizers and securely store host permissions across sessions.
  • Generate unique, cryptographically verifiable 3×9 Tambola/Housie ticket matrices.
  • Perform automated, server-side winning claim verification (e.g. Early Five, Lines, Full House).
  • Sync organizer credit balances and room history across devices.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or duplicate ticket exploitation during live games.

3. Third-Party Services & Authentication

We use trusted infrastructure providers to deliver our services:

  • Google OAuth: For identity verification and social sign-in. Data processed by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase (PostgreSQL & Auth): For encrypted database storage, real-time WebSocket game synchronization, and session management.
  • AWS Amplify / CloudFront: For secure global web hosting and CDN delivery with SSL/TLS encryption.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your profile and game history for as long as your account remains active. You may request complete deletion of your account, email address, and associated data at any time by emailing us at contact@dabhousie.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

5. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies on our databases, and secure tokenized authentication headers. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we encourage users to safeguard their device access.

6. Children's Privacy

DabHousie is intended for general family, social, and corporate entertainment. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parental consent.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
