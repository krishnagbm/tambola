Privacy Policy

Our commitment to transparency, data protection, and user rights.

🔒 Summary of Our Privacy Commitment

DabHousie collects minimal user data necessary to operate multiplayer Tambola games and authenticate event organizers. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks. We access authentication provider data (Google Identity, Sign in with Apple, Microsoft / Microsoft Entra ID, and verified corporate email OTP) solely for user authentication, organizer session management, and profile display.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the DabHousie platform, available at https://www.dabhousie.com, including all related subdomains, mobile web interfaces, and services operated by Digital App Studio ("we," "us," or "our").

DabHousie is a social multiplayer entertainment platform designed for family game nights, kitty parties, club gatherings, and corporate events to play 90-ball Tambola (also known as Housie or Indian Bingo) with digital cards, automated number draws, and automated server-side prize verification.

2. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

A. Identity & Single Sign-On (SSO) Providers (Google, Apple, Microsoft, & Corporate Email)

When you register or sign in as an event organizer, we support secure Single Sign-On (SSO) and passwordless authentication through industry-standard OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, and email verification providers. We request only the minimal public profile information required to identify your account and display your organizer badge:

Provider Data Fields Collected Source & Scope Purpose and Usage
Google Identity Email address, Display Name, Profile Photo URL, Subject ID (`sub`) Google OAuth Profile Scope (`email`, `profile`) Organizer authentication, session security, hosted game history recovery, and display in the organizer dashboard.
Sign in with Apple Apple Subject ID (`sub`), Email address (or Apple Private Relay alias `@privaterelay.appleid.com`), Name (shared once upon initial sign-in) Apple ID OAuth / OpenID Connect Scope (`name`, `email`) One-tap native and web organizer authentication. Fully respects Apple's "Hide My Email" privacy relay forwarding.
Microsoft / Microsoft Entra ID Email address, Display Name, Subject / Object ID (`sub` / `oid`) Microsoft OAuth / OpenID Connect Scope (`email`, `profile`, `openid`) Personal Microsoft account and corporate Microsoft 365 / Entra ID organizer authentication and domain verification.
Corporate Email OTP Email address and temporary one-time passcode Direct email OTP verification Passwordless organizer sign-in and domain verification for corporate branding without third-party SSO dependencies.

Note: We do NOT request, receive, or store your passwords, cloud storage files (Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud), contacts, calendars, or sensitive enterprise directory data.

B. Information Collected During Gameplay (Guests & Players)

  • Guest Session Identifier: Guest players who join games via invite codes or room links do not need to log in. We assign a pseudonymous session UUID stored locally in your browser to maintain your active game ticket, dabbed numbers, and live claim status.
  • Display Names & Avatars: Player nickname and avatar emoji selected by the user for in-game identification on the room leaderboard.
  • Game Activity & Logs: Live number call sequence, ticket generation records, timestamped prize claim requests (e.g., Early Five, Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Four Corners, Full House), and room completion statuses.

C. Technical & Device Information

  • Browser type, device operating system, viewport resolution, and IP address (processed temporarily by our CDN for network security and DDoS mitigation).

D. Corporate Brand Authorization Data & DVAA™ Telemetry

DabHousie features a proprietary Domain-Verified Automated Approval (DVAA™) architecture designed to provide zero-PII brand protection and prevent unauthorized trademark display. When an organizer customizes an event with corporate identity (organization name and domain-hosted logo), we process minimal verification telemetry:

  • Corporate Email & Domain Verification: When an organizer authenticates using a corporate domain email, we evaluate the domain matching to enable Instant Domain-Owner DVAA™ approval. Corporate approver email addresses are used strictly to deliver verification requests and official audit acknowledgements. Approver emails are never enrolled in marketing lists, sold, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Immutable Compliance Audit Records: To maintain brand integrity and prevent corporate impersonation, all DVAA™ operations—including auto-approvals, email dispatches, and web approvals—generate timestamped audit records containing event scale, organizer attribution, timestamp, verification IP, and approver email. Official audit records are automatically archived and delivered to DabHousie Compliance at contact@dabhousie.com.
  • Domain-Hosted Logo Fetching: Logos are loaded dynamically from the organization's official domain via HTTPS or validated through authorized CDN integrations (Logo.dev) without copying or storing third-party trademark files on DabHousie servers.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use your information strictly for the following legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authentication & Account Management: Authenticating game hosts, protecting organizer wallet credits, and restoring hosted game lobbies across devices.
  • Multiplayer Game Synchronization: Delivering live game state updates, caller numbers, and player lists in real time via WebSockets.
  • Automated Win Verification: Validating winning ticket claims on the server to prevent fraudulent or mistaken claims during live events.
  • Brand Verification & Compliance: Enabling corporate reviewers to authorize official branding and maintaining a verified audit trail.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Preventing bot spam, duplicate ticket exploitation, or denial-of-service attacks on live games.

4. Recent Games & Hall of Fame Public Display

Recent Games & Hall of Fame: If you win a prize in a public game, your chosen in-game nickname, avatar, and the prize won may be displayed on our public Recent Games page. We recommend choosing a nickname rather than your real name. Games marked Private Party by the host are never shown publicly. To request removal, email contact@dabhousie.com with your claim reference code.

4A. Brand Marketing Partner Data

When a business or marketing representative submits an offer through the Brand Marketing Partner Portal, we collect and process the following information:

  • Data Collected via Registration Form: Brand/company name, company website domain (used to dynamically resolve brand logos via Logo.dev), marketing representative name and role/title, corporate work email address, offer details (offer title, description and customer benefit, offer type, category, target country/region, currency, retail value, and reward sponsorship value / host cost), voucher batch quantity, product landing page URL, voucher code type and promotional code(s), and optional redemption rules (expiration date, redemption channel, minimum purchase, redemption restrictions, and new-customers-only eligibility).
  • Purpose & Retention: We collect this data to verify corporate identity, evaluate and configure sponsored reward campaigns in the DabHousie Partner Reward Catalog, deliver redemption details to verified game winners, and measure aggregate campaign impressions and landing-page clicks. Partner records are retained for the duration of the active campaign and historical showcase archive unless earlier deletion is requested.
  • Database Storage & Email Dispatch: Submitted brand partner registrations are stored in our encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL database (via the MPT_register_brand_offer service) and simultaneously trigger an automated confirmation and internal compliance notification email via Amazon SES. Direct partnership inquiries sent to partnerships@dabhousie.com are retained in our corporate mailbox for partner relationship management.
  • Intentional Public Display vs. Private Data: To fulfill the promotional purpose of the program, your brand name, domain-resolved logo, offer title, offer type, category, target region, currency, retail/sponsorship value, redemption rules, and landing page URL are intentionally displayed to game hosts, players, and visitors on our public Brand Partners and Recent Games pages. This is an intentional public promotional display of your offer, not a third-party data broker transfer. Actual promotional or voucher codes are stored privately and disclosed only to verified game winners. Marketing representative contact details (representative name, role, and corporate work email) are kept strictly internal and are never displayed publicly or sold to third parties.
  • Contact & Removal Rights: Brand representatives may request updates, deactivation, or complete deletion of their submitted contact data and partner offer listings at any time by emailing partnerships@dabhousie.com or contact@dabhousie.com. Verified removal requests are processed within 7 business days.

  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve the core features of the DabHousie application.

  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

5A. Apple & Microsoft Identity Disclosures

DabHousie respects user privacy across all supported authentication frameworks:

  • Sign in with Apple: Our integration complies fully with Apple's Developer Program License Agreement and Human Interface Guidelines. DabHousie natively honors Apple's "Hide My Email" Private Relay service (generating an anonymous @privaterelay.appleid.com address). We never track users across third-party websites or apps, and we do not share Apple user identifiers with advertising brokers or analytics aggregators.
  • Microsoft Identity Platform (Microsoft Entra ID & Personal Accounts): When signing in with a Microsoft or work account, we request only standard OpenID Connect identity claims (openid, profile, email). We do not request access to your organization's Microsoft 365 files, Outlook mailbox, Teams chats, or Entra directory graph beyond your basic user sign-in profile.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Infrastructure

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following essential cloud infrastructure subprocessors who are contractually bound to confidentiality and security standards:

  • Supabase Inc. (Database & Authentication): Encrypted PostgreSQL database and authentication engine hosted on secure AWS infrastructure with Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS Amplify / CloudFront / SES): Secure HTTPS web hosting, SSL/TLS certificate management, Content Delivery Network (CDN), and automated transactional email audit deliveries.
  • Google LLC (Google Identity Services): OAuth 2.0 authentication provider for Google accounts.
  • Apple Inc. (Sign in with Apple): Authentication provider for Apple ID and Private Relay email forwarding.
  • Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Identity Platform / Entra ID): OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect authentication provider for personal Microsoft and organizational work accounts.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

DabHousie uses browser local storage (`localStorage`) and session cookies strictly for essential functional operations:

  • mpt_player_name & mpt_player_avatar: Preserving your customized player name and avatar preference locally.
  • mpt_local_uuid: Anonymous guest player session persistence across page refreshes.
  • sb-access-token / sb-refresh-token: Secure encrypted authentication tokens for logged-in hosts.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or cross-site marketing trackers.

8. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights (Right to be Forgotten)

We retain user profile data and game history for as long as your account remains active. You have full control over your personal data:

  • Request Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account, email address, profile, and hosted game history at any time by emailing us at contact@dabhousie.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We process all verified deletion requests within 7 business days.
  • Revoke Google Account Access: You can revoke DabHousie's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Permissions Settings.
  • Revoke Apple ID Access: You can manage or revoke DabHousie's access to your Apple ID at any time through your Apple device settings (Settings > Apple ID > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple) or at appleid.apple.com.
  • Revoke Microsoft Account Access: You can manage or revoke DabHousie's access to your Microsoft account at any time at account.live.com/consent/Manage (for personal accounts) or myapps.microsoft.com (for organizational work accounts).

9. Data Security & Encryption

We employ robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS protocols.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records, auth tokens, and game configurations are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Granular database access policies enforce that only authorized organizers can access their private room data.

10. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR)

DabHousie is designed for general family, social, and corporate recreational party entertainment. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information directly from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, please contact us at contact@dabhousie.com, and we will promptly remove the information.
