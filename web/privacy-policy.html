🔒 Summary of Our Privacy Commitment

DabHousie collects minimal user data necessary to operate multiplayer Tambola games and authenticate event organizers. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks. We access Google OAuth data solely for user authentication, organizer identity, and profile display.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the DabHousie platform, available at https://www.dabhousie.com, including all related subdomains, mobile web interfaces, and services operated by Digital App Studio ("we," "us," or "our").

DabHousie is a social multiplayer entertainment platform designed for family game nights, kitty parties, club gatherings, and corporate events to play 90-ball Tambola (also known as Housie or Indian Bingo) with digital cards, automated number draws, and automated server-side prize verification.

2. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

A. Google User Data (Google OAuth Sign-In)

When you choose to sign in or register as a game organizer using Google Sign-In, we request access to your standard Google public profile and email address. Specifically, we receive and store:

Data Field Source Purpose and Usage
Google Email Address Google Profile Scope (`email`) Unique account identification, organizer session authentication, recovery of hosted game history, and delivery of magic login links (if requested).
Full Name / Display Name Google Profile Scope (`profile`) Populating the initial in-game organizer and player display name in game lobbies (can be edited at any time in profile settings).
Profile Avatar URL Google Profile Scope (`profile`) Displaying your avatar icon in the navigation bar, organizer dashboard, and profile view.
Google Account ID (`sub`) Google OAuth Provider Secure cryptographic user ID mapping in our database to ensure seamless sign-in across browser sessions.

Note: We do NOT request, receive, or store your Google password, Google Drive files, contacts, calendar, or any sensitive scopes.

B. Information Collected During Gameplay (Guests & Players)

  • Guest Session Identifier: Guest players who join games via invite codes or room links do not need to log in. We assign a pseudonymous session UUID stored locally in your browser to maintain your active game ticket, dabbed numbers, and live claim status.
  • Display Names & Avatars: Player nickname and avatar emoji selected by the user for in-game identification on the room leaderboard.
  • Game Activity & Logs: Live number call sequence, ticket generation records, timestamped prize claim requests (e.g., Early Five, Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Four Corners, Full House), and room completion statuses.

C. Technical & Device Information

  • Browser type, device operating system, viewport resolution, and IP address (processed temporarily by our CDN for network security and DDoS mitigation).

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use your information strictly for the following legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authentication & Account Management: Authenticating game hosts, protecting organizer wallet credits, and restoring hosted game lobbies across devices.
  • Multiplayer Game Synchronization: Delivering live game state updates, caller numbers, and player lists in real time via WebSockets.
  • Automated Win Verification: Validating winning ticket claims on the server to prevent fraudulent or mistaken claims during live events.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Preventing bot spam, duplicate ticket exploitation, or denial-of-service attacks on live games.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

DabHousie's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve the core features of the DabHousie application.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not permit humans to read Google user data unless we have obtained your explicit agreement for troubleshooting, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required to comply with applicable law.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Infrastructure

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following essential cloud infrastructure subprocessors who are contractually bound to confidentiality and security standards:

  • Supabase Inc. (Database & Authentication): Encrypted PostgreSQL database and authentication engine hosted on secure AWS infrastructure with Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS Amplify / CloudFront): Secure HTTPS web hosting, SSL/TLS certificate management, and Content Delivery Network (CDN).
  • Google LLC (Google Identity Services): OAuth 2.0 authentication provider.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

DabHousie uses browser local storage (`localStorage`) and session cookies strictly for essential functional operations:

  • mpt_player_name & mpt_player_avatar: Preserving your customized player name and avatar preference locally.
  • mpt_local_uuid: Anonymous guest player session persistence across page refreshes.
  • sb-access-token / sb-refresh-token: Secure encrypted authentication tokens for logged-in hosts.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or cross-site marketing trackers.

7. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights (Right to be Forgotten)

We retain user profile data and game history for as long as your account remains active. You have full control over your personal data:

  • Request Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account, email address, profile, and hosted game history at any time by emailing us at contact@dabhousie.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We process all verified deletion requests within 7 business days.
  • Revoke Google Account Access: You can revoke DabHousie's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Permissions Settings.

8. Data Security & Encryption

We employ robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS protocols.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records, auth tokens, and game configurations are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Granular database access policies enforce that only authorized organizers can access their private room data.

9. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR)

DabHousie is designed for general family, social, and corporate recreational party entertainment. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information directly from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, please contact us at contact@dabhousie.com, and we will promptly remove the information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, security enhancements, or legal requirements. When updates occur, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of DabHousie after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
