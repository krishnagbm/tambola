Frequently Asked Questions

What is DabHousie?

DabHousie is a live multiplayer and solo skill platform for Classic 90-Ball Bingo (Tambola, Housie) and DabHousie™ Skill Specials (FlashHousie™, MakeHousie™, FixHousie™).

Is DabHousie free to play?

Yes, DabHousie offers free Solo Skill Practice and a free tier for family multiplayer games up to 5 players (0 credits required)*. For larger groups (6 to 250+ players), organizers can purchase credit packs on dabhousie.com/pricing.html.

Do players need to download an app to join?

No app download is required for guests. Players can join instantly on any web browser.

How does automated prize claim verification work?

When a player claims a winning pattern, DabHousie's server automatically validates the ticket numbers against the drawn numbers in real time.
