DabHousie - Multiplayer Tambola, Housie & 90-Ball Bingo

DabHousie is a modern social multiplayer entertainment platform created by Digital App Studio. Designed for family game nights, kitty parties, club gatherings, and corporate events, DabHousie makes it easy to host and play live 90-ball Tambola (also known as Housie or Indian Bingo) with digital cards, automated number calling, and server-side prize claim verification.

🎮 Zero-Friction Play

Players and guests can join any live room instantly via invite link or room code with no registration required.

🎙️ Host & Schedule

Organizers can sign in with Google to schedule game nights, host private parties, and manage player capacities.

⚡ Automated Verification

Instant server-side validation for Early Five, Lines, Four Corners, and Full House eliminates party disputes.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Free Family Fun

Always 100% free for small family games up to 5 players with unlimited play and full feature access.
