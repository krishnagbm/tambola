GAME RULES & OVERVIEW

The Ultimate 90-Ball Bingo & Tambola Guide

Whether your group knows it as Tambola, Housie, or 90-Ball Bingo, explore the complete guide to ticket structure, winning patterns, and modern digital hosting.

🌐 One Game, Global Names

The classic 90-ball number drawing game is played by millions around the globe. While the name changes depending on where you live, the gameplay and excitement remain universal:

US & UK 90-Ball Bingo

Popular across the United States and the United Kingdom, played in halls, fundraisers, and social community nights.

India & Asia Tambola

The beloved social game across India and Southeast Asia, ubiquitous at Diwali gatherings, kitty parties, and club galas.

Australia & NZ Housie

Commonly played in Australia and New Zealand as community fundraisers, charity games, and social club events.

🎫 3×9 Ticket Architecture

A genuine 90-ball bingo ticket contains exactly 15 numbers arranged within a 3-row by 9-column grid (27 total cells):

  • Each row contains exactly 5 numbers and 4 blank spaces.
  • Numbers are organized in strict column order:
    • Column 1: Numbers 1 to 9
    • Column 2: Numbers 10 to 19
    • Column 3: Numbers 20 to 29
    • Column 4: Numbers 30 to 39
    • Column 5: Numbers 40 to 49
    • Column 6: Numbers 50 to 59
    • Column 7: Numbers 60 to 69
    • Column 8: Numbers 70 to 79
    • Column 9: Numbers 80 to 90

DabHousie generates mathematically verified unique tickets so no two players in the same room ever hold identical winning combinations.

🏆 Standard Winning Combinations

Games can be configured with multiple winning patterns to keep the excitement high throughout the event:

⚡ Early Five (Jaldi 5)

Awarded to the first player to dab any 5 numbers anywhere on their ticket.

📌 Four Corners

Awarded for crossing off the leftmost and rightmost numbers on the top and bottom rows.

🥇 Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line

Individual prizes awarded for completing all 5 numbers in a specific horizontal row.

👑 Full House (Bingo!)

The grand prize awarded to the player who crosses off all 15 numbers on their ticket.
