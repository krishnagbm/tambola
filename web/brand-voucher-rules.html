TRUST, INTEGRITY & BRAND SPONSOR GUARD

Brand Partner Gift Voucher Allocation Rulebook

To protect our Brand Sponsors and ensure that high-value marketing gifts are delivered only to genuine, engaged audiences, DabHousie enforces strict server-authoritative allocation rules. This guarantees brand partners get high-intent customer acquisition while keeping game tournaments fun and fair.

RULE 1 • PER-GAME BRAND CAP

🏷️ Limit of 1 Free Sponsored Voucher per Game per Brand

A host cannot attach multiple 100% Free sponsored vouchers from the same brand across different prize patterns (e.g., Early 5, Top Line, and Full House cannot all feature the same brand's free voucher in the same room).

Why this protects sponsors: Prevents any single host from exhausting a brand sponsor's entire monthly promotional pool in one or two private sessions. Organizers are encouraged to combine diverse brand templates or host-funded gifts.
RULE 2 • VALUE SCALED TO AUDIENCE SIZE

📈 Voucher Face Value is Capped by Room Capacity

Free games (1–5 players) are completely ineligible for 100% Free Sponsored Brand Vouchers. For funded rooms, the maximum face value of any sponsored voucher is strictly proportional to the number of seats:

Room Tier / Capacity Host Credits Cost Max Allowed Free Voucher Value Status
Family Pack (1–5 Players) 0 Credits (Free) $0 (Not Eligible) Custom Gifts / Global Templates only
Small Party (6–15 Players) 15 Credits Up to $10.00 Eligible for $5–$10 Brand Pilots
Medium Group (16–25 Players) 25 Credits Up to $25.00 Eligible for $15–$25 Brand Vouchers
Large Group (26–50 Players) 50 Credits Up to $50.00 Eligible for $25–$50 Brand Vouchers
Club & Mega (51–250+ Players) 100–250+ Credits Up to $100.00+ Eligible for Premium Hampers & Experiences
RULE 3 • ACTIVE TICKET PARTICIPATION QUORUM

👥 75% Active Ticket Participation Required to Settle / Allocate

A room configured for a paid capacity tier must reach at least 75% active participant strength when the game concludes naturally. Crucially, at least 75% of players must have actively filled in / marked numbers on their tickets during the game—passive spectator joins, idle tabs, or non-active registrations do not qualify.

Anti-Dummy / Anti-Ghost Quorum: Simply registering or idling in a 15-player room with 2 or 3 active dabbers does not satisfy the 75% active participation quorum (at least 12 active players who filled in their tickets are required for 15 seats). In such cases, the system locks sponsored vouchers from distribution during claims and allocation to prevent sham game extraction.
RULE 4 • GAMEPLAY INTEGRITY

🏁 Allocation Only After Natural Game Conclusion

Free gift vouchers are officially allocated and delivered only after the game has concluded naturally through verified gameplay, where all enabled winning patterns (e.g., Jaldi 5, Lines, and Full House) have been legitimately claimed and audited.

Anti-Speedrun Guard: Abruptly terminated, abandoned, or prematurely ended rooms cannot award or unlock brand promotional vouchers.
RULE 5 • IDENTITY INTEGRITY

🔐 Allocation Restricted to Registered Accounts Only

Free sponsored gift vouchers are strictly reserved for authenticated, registered players. While temporary guest players may join and enjoy casual gameplay, they cannot receive or unlock Brand Partner sponsored vouchers until they verify their registered account with a valid email.

Why this matters to Brands: Brand partners sponsor vouchers to acquire real prospective customers. Guest accounts with disposable browser storage do not provide accountable customer attribution or redemption tracking.
RULE 6 • ANTI-SYBIL & COOLDOWN LIMITS

🛡️ Anti-Sybil Protection & Winner Redemption Cooldown

To prevent collusion or duplicate-device exploitation:

  • Unique Device / Network Fingerprint: Multiple player instances originating from the same machine or duplicate browser windows do not increment the 75% active participation quorum.
  • Winner Cooldown: An individual registered player may win/claim a free sponsored reward from the same brand at most once every 30 days.
Host Confidence: Honest organizers can host corporate and party games with complete peace of mind, knowing that legitimate winners receive legitimate brand rewards while fraud vectors are fully defended.
