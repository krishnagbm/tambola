STEP-BY-STEP PLATFORM GUIDE

How DabHousie Works

Whether you are hosting a 5-person family party or a 250-player corporate gala,
DabHousie automates ticket distribution, number calling, and server prize validation.

👑 For Game Organizers (Hosts)

Organizing a live game takes less than 60 seconds with zero preparation, printing, or app installs required.

1. Easy Setup & Game Scheduling

Choose your room name, select group capacity (1–250 players), configure winning patterns, and launch immediately or schedule the game in advance for a future date and time.

2. Display QR Code & Instant Links

Display a large, high-resolution room QR code on your TV, screen, or printout for instant phone scanning, or share a 6-digit room code and clickable link over WhatsApp, chat, or email.

3. Automated Speech Caller & Board

Draw numbers automatically with realistic speech calling at customizable speeds, or draw manually while projecting the live 1–90 board to a smart TV or projector.

4. Live Host Control & Instant Verification

Monitor connected players in real time, manage ticket distribution, and let DabHousie's server-side engine automatically validate prize claims without pausing game momentum.

Live Organizer Control Screen with Auto-Pilot Timer, 1–90 Master Board & Real-Time Claims Roster

📱 For Players & Guests

We believe joining a party game should be effortless and frictionless for every guest.

Zero App Downloads

Guests open the link on Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. No installation required to play.

No Account Required

Players simply enter a nickname, select a fun avatar, and get their unique digital ticket immediately.

Real-Time Dabbing

Tap numbers as they are called or turn on auto-dabbing. The game highlights winning patterns when achieved.

Instant Mobile Browser Play (Safari/Chrome) — 3×9 ticket, caller counter, and real-time claim buttons

🛡️ Server-Side Prize Claim Verification

Traditional paper games often suffer from disputed claims, missed calls, or false shouts. DabHousie automatically validates prize claims with instant server verification.

⚡ Real-Time Logic Engine

When a player taps Claim Prize (e.g., Top Line or Full House), our database engine verifies the player's ticket numbers against the sequence of numbers drawn so far.

🎟️ Scannable QR Prize Vouchers

Validated winners receive an official digital voucher and QR code on their screen, making in-person physical prize hand-outs seamless at clubhouses and parties.

Multiplayer live sync: instantaneous server prize validation, winning alerts, and bogey protection

📺 Big-Screen TV & Projector Mode

Hosting an in-person event at a hall, clubhouse, or living room? Open DabHousie's dedicated Live Display link on your smart TV or projector:

  • On-Screen Room QR Code: Guests walk in and simply scan the TV screen with their smartphone camera to join instantly.
  • High-Visibility 1–90 Number Board: Highlights called numbers with bold, high-contrast colors.
  • Last Called Number Callout: Oversized display visible from across the room.
  • Live Winners Ticker: Automatically celebrates winners in real time.

Live Big-Screen TV Display — glowing 1–90 board, active caller animation, and real-time winner notifications

Whether organizing society gatherings, festive kitty parties, or corporate galas, DabHousie is designed to support large lobbies with fast real-time synchronization and automated prize validation.

⚡ Fast Instant Verification

Instant server-side validation handles simultaneous claims without pauses or lag across 50, 100, or 250 concurrent connected players.
