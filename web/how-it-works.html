STEP-BY-STEP PLATFORM GUIDE

How DabHousie Works

Whether hosting a 250-player company all-hands or playing a casual living room game with family,
DabHousie makes live Tambola, Housie, and 90-Ball Bingo simple and automated.
Explore the steps below or tap any section to view details.

Quick Jump:

Organizing a live game takes less than 60 seconds with zero preparation, printing, or app installs required. The host retains total control over room access, speed, and prize claims.

1. Easy Setup & Game Scheduling

Choose your room name, select group capacity (up to 250 players), configure winning patterns, choose Quick Dabby or Classic calling, and launch immediately or schedule for a future kickoff.

2. Display QR Code & Instant Links

Display a large, high-resolution room QR code on your TV, screen, or printout for instant phone scanning, or share a 6-digit room code and clickable link over WhatsApp, chat, or email.

3. Automated Speech Caller & Board

Draw numbers automatically with realistic speech calling at customizable speeds, or draw manually while projecting the live 1–90 board to a smart TV, auditorium display, or Zoom call.

4. Live Host Control & Instant Verification

Monitor connected players in real time, manage ticket distribution, and let DabHousie's server-side engine automatically validate prize claims without pausing game momentum—so the host can play too!

In traditional 90-ball bingo with smaller groups (under 25 players), over 60% of numbers drawn are "dead calls" that nobody holds, causing games to drag. Quick Dabby™ solves this completely:

⚡ Active-Ticket Pool Selection

Quick Dabby inspects all tickets issued to active players in the room and draws numbers strictly from that combined unique pool (e.g. 44 numbers for a 4-player game), ensuring almost every draw dabs a ticket!

⏱️ Concludes in 5–8 Minutes

Reduces total calls needed from 75–85 down to ~35–45 draws, cutting game duration in half so it fits corporate meeting agendas and keeps kids and elders fully energized.

🔄 Seamless Lobby Toggle

Hosts can switch between Quick Dabby ⚡ and Classic 1–90 🎲 right from the Organizer Game Lobby before launching the game.

🛡️ 100% Authentic Rules

All classic winning patterns (Early 5, Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Full House) and cryptographic ticket validation remain identical and authentic.

Keep the energy soaring right after the final Full House is claimed! Instead of the event abruptly ending, DabHousie launches an integrated interactive Trivia round:

🎯 Seamless One-Click Transition

No need to ask guests to switch to Kahoot or Slido. The game automatically transitions directly to the trivia countdown on player screens and stage displays.

⏱️ Live Synchronous Countdown

Questions appear simultaneously on big-screen TVs and mobile phones with a high-energy ticking countdown clock and instant answer locking.

🏆 Celebratory Top 5 Podium

Real-time scoring based on speed and accuracy reveals the 1st through 5th place winners on a vibrant animated victory podium.

💼 Custom Business or Family Content

Hosts can configure questions based on quarterly OKRs, company history, all-hands announcements, or fun family trivia moments.

Traditional paper games often suffer from disputed claims, missed calls, or false shouts. DabHousie automatically validates prize claims with instant server verification.

⚡ Real-Time Logic Engine

When a player taps Claim Prize (e.g., Top Line or Full House), our database engine verifies the player's ticket numbers against the sequence of numbers drawn so far in milliseconds.

🎟️ Scannable QR Prize Vouchers

Validated winners receive an official digital voucher and QR code on their screen, making in-person physical prize hand-outs or corporate gift voucher claims seamless.
