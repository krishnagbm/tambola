🛡️ Executive & HR Compliance FAQ

Does this comply with corporate anti-gambling policies?

100% Yes. DabHousie has zero cash collection, zero wagering, and zero cash payouts. Prizes are fulfilled exclusively as branded merchant gift cards and discount vouchers. It is classified strictly as a social team-building engagement tool.

Can our corporate firewall block it?

No. DabHousie operates over standard HTTPS/WSS (WebSockets) ports 443 with modern enterprise SSL encryption. No browser extensions, native executables, or third-party permissions are ever required.

What if half the team shows up late?

Your budget is completely safe. With DabHousie's intelligent Quorum Control, if player check-in does not hit your required quorum, the game automatically reschedules and your room credits remain untouched in your wallet.

Can we display our company logo on the screen?

Yes. With DVAA™ White-Label Identity, corporate organizers can display their company logo across digital tickets, the waiting room lobby, and the `/live` big-screen TV projector broadcast.
