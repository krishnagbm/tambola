🛡️ Executive & HR Compliance FAQ

Does DabHousie align with corporate entertainment and non-gambling policies?

DabHousie is designed for social and group entertainment without cash wagering or cash payouts. Rewards are limited to optional brand merchant vouchers and custom non-cash items, with no player buy-ins. Organizers should follow applicable company policies and local laws.

How does DabHousie run on corporate networks?

DabHousie runs in modern web browsers over standard HTTPS and secure WebSocket (WSS) connections (port 443). It requires no desktop executables, mobile app installations, or browser plugins.

What happens if attendance is lower than expected at start time?

Game credits are deducted only when an event actually starts. For scheduled games, if confirmed attendance is below the configured quorum at kickoff, the organizer receives an alert with a decision window to reschedule, proceed, or cancel before any game credits are deducted.

Can we display our company logo during the event?

Yes. Corporate hosts authenticating with a verified organization domain can activate Domain-Verified Automated Approval (DVAA™) to display their company logo on digital player tickets, the waiting lobby, and the big-screen TV / projector display.
