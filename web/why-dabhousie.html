🛡️ Executive & HR Compliance FAQ

Does DabHousie align with corporate non-gambling policies?

DabHousie is designed specifically to support corporate event policies and host-defined recognition. It operates strictly without cash wagering, player buy-in pools, or cash payouts. Hosts determine voucher values in accordance with their corporate policies and retain full control over prize-claim approvals.

What is Quick Dabby mode and when should we use it?

Quick Dabby is an intelligent draw mode for high-tempo corporate events, all-hands meetings, and lunch-and-learns. Instead of calling randomly from all 90 numbers when a small group plays, Quick Dabby draws exclusively from numbers actually held by participating colleagues, concluding up to 50% faster with zero dead calls.

How does the Corporate Post-Game Recall Trivia round work?

Recall Trivia lets organizers reinforce meeting announcements, company history, quarterly OKRs, or training points immediately after Tambola. Questions and live countdowns appear synchronously on big-screen displays and mobile phones with real-time scoring and a celebratory Top 5 Podium—without needing external tools like Kahoot or Slido.

How does DabHousie run on corporate networks?

DabHousie runs on standard web connectivity over HTTPS and secure WebSocket (WSS) on port 443. It requires no desktop executables, mobile app installations, or browser extensions, fitting standard corporate security and firewall configurations.

What happens if attendance is lower than expected at kickoff?

Game credits are deducted only when an event actually begins. For scheduled games, if confirmed attendance is below the configured quorum at start time, the organizer receives an alert to reschedule, adjust, or cancel before any game credits are deducted. Host credit packs have a 1-year validity.

Can we display our corporate logo during the event?

Yes. Corporate hosts authenticating with a verified organization email can activate Domain-Verified Automated Approval (DVAA™) to display their company logo on digital player tickets, the waiting lobby, and the big-screen projector view.

🎉 Family & Party Host FAQ

Can we really play with our family for free?

Yes! DabHousie provides free casual family play for up to 5 players per room without requiring a credit card, subscription, or trial period. For larger family reunions, birthdays, or festival parties, affordable one-time host packs start at just $10 with no recurring fees.

Do my grandparents or young kids need to download an app?

Not at all! DabHousie is 100% web-based. Simply share your game link via WhatsApp, SMS, or iMessage. When they tap the link on their phone, tablet, or laptop, their ticket opens directly in their browser without installing anything.

How can we display the game on our Living Room TV?

Open DabHousie on your Smart TV browser or cast it from your laptop or tablet using AirPlay or Google Cast. The TV acts as the grand caller board with real-time numbers and voice calls, while everyone sits comfortably on the sofa dabbing on their own phones!

What is Quick Dabby and why is it great for families?

In standard 90-ball games with 6–12 family members, over 70% of numbers called are "dead numbers" that nobody has. Quick Dabby automatically filters the pool to only numbers held on active family tickets. Games finish in a brisk, exciting 6–8 minutes so nobody gets bored.

Can cousins or relatives join from other cities or countries?

Yes! Because DabHousie synchronizes live over secure cloud servers, relatives across different cities, states, or timezones can play together seamlessly with zero lag.

Are there real money prizes or gambling?

No. DabHousie is strictly non-gambling and 100% safe for all ages. Family hosts can give out chocolates, family bragging rights, or fun gift cards if they wish, but the app itself has zero cash betting, buy-in pots, or real-money payouts.
