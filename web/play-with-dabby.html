⚡ What is "Play with Dabby"?

Play with Dabby is DabHousie's free, solo rapid-calling practice arena. It allows visitors and players to experience genuine 90-ball Tambola gameplay directly in the browser—with instant automatic calling, countdown timer, and live ticket interaction—without needing an account or waiting for a live scheduled event.

🎟️ The Authentic 3×9 Ticket Architecture

A standard 90-ball Tambola ticket consists of a 3-row by 9-column grid (27 total cells). Exactly 15 cells contain numbers (exactly 5 numbers per row) and 12 cells remain blank (exactly 4 blanks per row). This precise mathematical balance ensures fair chance and pattern parity across every ticket.

📊 How Numbers are Distributed Across Columns

Numbers 1 through 90 are grouped strictly into 9 column decades from left to right:

• Col 1: 1–9  |  Col 2: 10–19  |  Col 3: 20–29
• Col 4: 30–39  |  Col 5: 40–49  |  Col 6: 50–59
• Col 7: 60–69  |  Col 8: 70–79  |  Col 9: 80–90

Each column holds between 1 and 3 numbers, always sorted in strictly ascending numerical order from top to bottom.

📐 Why Generated Tickets are Mathematically Valid

DabHousie generates tickets using a constraint-satisfaction generation engine. Every ticket satisfies three simultaneous invariants: (1) exactly 5 numbers per row, (2) 1 to 3 numbers per column within decade bounds, and (3) strictly sorted ascending column order. Out of over 8.1 trillion possible combinations, no two players receive identical tickets.

🎱 How Called Numbers Relate to the Ticket

As Dabby calls numbers at random from the 1–90 pool, each drawn ball is highlighted in the caller sphere. In this practice mode, you have 5 seconds per call to scan your ticket's corresponding decade column, locate the matching number if present, and dab it before the next ball arrives.

🎯 How Dabbing & Reflex Training Works

Tapping a called number on your ticket "dabs" it—turning the tile gold and increasing your dab score. Dabbing reinforces spatial recognition: knowing that number 47 is always in Column 5 or 83 is in Column 9 trains you to find numbers in under two seconds during fast-paced multiplayer games.

🏆 Winning Patterns in Tambola & Housie

In standard Tambola and 90-ball Bingo, players race to complete specific patterns:

• Early 5 (Jaldi 5): First 5 numbers dabbed anywhere on the ticket.
• Top Line / Middle Line / Bottom Line: All 5 numbers on any individual row.
• Four Corners: The leftmost & rightmost numbers of the top and bottom rows.
• Full House (Bingo): All 15 numbers completely dabbed on the ticket!
