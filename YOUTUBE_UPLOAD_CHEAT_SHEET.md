## Step 6: YouTube Review Bot Compliance Note (Bypassing 18+ Restrictions)

YouTube's automated multi-modal machine learning classifiers scan spoken audio transcripts, on-screen text, titles, and descriptions. Because games of 90-Ball Bingo, Tambola, and Housie inherently mention *"numbers"*, *"tickets"*, *"claims"*, and *"prizes"*, automated bots can mistakenly classify the content as real-money gambling or simulated gambling, triggering age restrictions (18+).


### Critical Rules for Review Bot Compliance:
1. **Never use negative keyword disclaimers**:
   - **Do NOT write**: *"No gambling, no betting, no real money wagering."*
   - YouTube's automated keyword extractors do not reliably recognize semantic negation (the word "no") and will flag videos for containing the word *"gambling"*.
2. **Audience Configuration**:
   - Set **"Audience"** to: *"No, it's not made for kids"* (standard general audience).
   - Under **Age restriction (advanced)**: Ensure *"No, don't restrict my video to viewers over 18 only"* is selected.
