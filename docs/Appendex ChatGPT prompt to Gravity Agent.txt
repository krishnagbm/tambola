FINAL HOMEPAGE MESSAGING & POSITIONING UPDATE

Please use this as the final messaging direction for the DabHousie static HTML homepage.

Do not redesign the entire site. Do not change the actual game functionality. This task is primarily about improving the homepage's first impression, messaging hierarchy, SEO wording, and CTA clarity.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT POSITIONING

DabHousie is a live multiplayer:

* Tambola
* Housie
* 90-Ball Bingo

These terms are important because different audiences know the game by different names.

For U.S. visitors in particular, "90-Ball Bingo" should be clearly visible because "Tambola" and "Housie" may not immediately communicate what the game is.

Do NOT treat 90-Ball Bingo as a different product. The messaging should communicate that DabHousie is the same style of game commonly known as Tambola, Housie, or 90-Ball Bingo.

PRIMARY HOMEPAGE OBJECTIVE

A first-time visitor should understand DabHousie within approximately 3–4 seconds.

They should immediately understand:

1. What is this?
   → A live multiplayer Tambola / Housie / 90-Ball Bingo game.

2. What can I do?
   → Join a game or host a game.

3. Can I play immediately?
   → Yes, players can join through the web without installing an app.

4. Why would I want the mobile app?
   → For convenient anytime access, scheduled games and push reminders.

The homepage should sell the EXPERIENCE first and explain features second.

## SECTION 1 — HERO

Use:

DabHousie

H1:

"Play Tambola. Connect. Win."

Supporting text:

"Live multiplayer Tambola, Housie & 90-Ball Bingo for friends, family, parties and events."

PRIMARY CTA:

"Join a Game"

SECONDARY CTA:

"Host a Game"

Under the buttons, use:

"Join instantly on the web — no app required."

IMPORTANT:

"Join a Game" must be the visually dominant CTA.

Do NOT use "Play Now" as the primary CTA.

The reason is that "Join a Game" immediately communicates the multiplayer nature of DabHousie and makes sense to a visitor who has received a game invitation/code.

"Host a Game" provides the natural second path for organizers.

Use natural language rather than repeatedly repeating the same keywords.

The goal is to make the site understandable to both:

* people searching for "Tambola"
* people searching for "Housie"
* people searching for "90-Ball Bingo"

## CRITICAL FIRST-SCREEN TEST

After implementation, look at the homepage as if you have NEVER heard of DabHousie.

Within 3–4 seconds, the visitor should be able to answer:

WHAT IS IT?

"Tambola / Housie / 90-Ball Bingo."

WHAT IS DIFFERENT?

"Live multiplayer with my group."

WHAT CAN I DO?

"Join a Game or Host a Game."

DO I NEED AN APP?

"No. I can join on the web."

WHY GET THE APP?

"For anytime access, scheduled games and push reminders."

If these answers are not immediately obvious visually, simplify the hero rather than adding more text.

---

## DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Do not turn the homepage into a wall of SEO text.

The hierarchy should be:

1. GAME
2. SOCIAL EXPERIENCE
3. JOIN / HOST
4. HOW IT WORKS
5. MOBILE APP BENEFITS
6. FEATURES
7. SEO / DETAILED INFORMATION

The visitor should feel:

"That's a Tambola/Housie game I can play with my people."

not:

"This is a software platform with many features."

Keep DabHousie fun, social, modern and easy to understand.

Finally, inspect the finished page on both desktop and mobile and verify that "Join a Game" is clearly the primary CTA and that "90-Ball Bingo" is visible without requiring the visitor to scroll far down the page.
